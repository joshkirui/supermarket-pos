POS SYSTEM — Pitching Script

Word-by-word script for presenting the POS system to investors, partners, or supermarket owners

Estimated time: 12–15 minutes | 12 slides

SLIDE 1: Opening

📊 Slide: Cover — POS System
"Good [morning/afternoon], everyone. Thank you for your time today.

My name is [Your Name], and I'm here to show you something that will change how supermarkets operate in Kenya.

Let me ask you a quick question — how many of you have stood in a supermarket queue, watching the cashier write things down by hand, or fumble with a calculator?

[pause for hands]

That's exactly the problem we've solved."
Tip: Make eye contact during the question. This engages the audience immediately.

SLIDE 2: The Problem

📊 Slide: The Problem
"Right now, thousands of supermarkets across Kenya are running on paper, spreadsheets, or outdated systems that weren't built for this market.

The consequences are real:

First — manual processes. Handwritten receipts. Manual stock counts. When was the last time you saw a shopkeeper counting rice by hand? That's not efficiency. That's time and money walking out the door.

Second — zero real-time visibility. A supermarket owner has no idea what's selling, what's running low, or how much cash is in the register — until the end of the day. By then, it's too late.

Third — payment friction. Kenya leads the world in mobile money. 73% of our GDP flows through M-Pesa. But most POS systems treat it as an afterthought — an expensive add-on, not a core feature.

And the numbers tell the story: Kenya loses an estimated 2.5 billion shillings annually to inventory shrinkage and poor stock management alone."

SLIDE 3: The Solution

📊 Slide: Our Solution
"So what did we build?

We built a complete, modern point-of-sale system designed specifically for Kenyan supermarkets.

Let me walk you through what makes it different:

Barcode scanning — you point the scanner, the product pops up instantly. No typing, no errors. The cashier just scans and goes.

M-Pesa STK Push — the customer enters their phone number, the payment prompt appears on their phone, they enter their PIN, and the system confirms in seconds. No separate M-Pesa machine needed.

Real-time inventory — the moment a product is scanned and sold, the stock level updates. You always know what you have.

Cash management — every shilling is tracked. Opening float, cash in, cash out, expenses — all recorded automatically.

And here's what's critical for us: it's built for Kenya from day one. KES currency. 16% VAT. KRA compliant. M-Pesa native. This isn't an imported system we're retrofitting — it was born here."

SLIDE 4: Key Features

📊 Slide: Key Features (dark slide)
"Let me show you the eight pillars of this system:

One — POS Terminal. Barcode scanner integration with auto-focus. The input is always ready. Scan and go.

Two — Inventory. Stock tracking, low-stock alerts with amber warnings, inter-branch transfers, damage logging. Everything moves with the product.

Three — Cash Management. Every till session is tracked. Cashiers open with a float, the system records every sale, every cash in, every cash out. End-of-day reconciliation takes minutes, not hours.

Four — Reports. Department-level reports. Sales analytics. Top products. And every report is printable with one click.

Five — Customers. Built-in loyalty program. Bronze, Silver, Gold tiers. Points earned and redeemed automatically.

Six — M-Pesa. Native STK Push integration. Not an add-on. Built into the payment flow from the start.

Seven — Security. Role-based access. Every user logs in with a PIN. Every action is audited.

Eight — Desktop App. Electron packaging means it runs as a native desktop app on Windows, Mac, or Linux."

SLIDE 5: Technical Architecture

📊 Slide: Technical Architecture
"Now let me give you a quick look under the hood — but don't worry, I'll keep it simple.

The backend runs on NestJS, one of the most robust Node.js frameworks. It has 15 modular controllers and over 60 API endpoints. Every feature — sales, inventory, customers, payments — is its own module. Clean, maintainable, scalable.

The database is PostgreSQL — the same database used by banks and governments worldwide. We have 30+ relational tables with full ACID compliance. Your data is safe.

The frontend is React with Tailwind CSS — a modern, responsive interface. And it's wrapped in Electron, so it runs as a desktop application, not just a website.

And the most important architectural decision: it works offline. If the internet goes down — and in Kenya, it will — the system keeps running. Sales are recorded locally and synced when the connection returns. You never lose a sale."

SLIDE 6: API & Database

📊 Slide: API Endpoints / Database Schema
"Let me put some numbers to this.

We have over 60 REST API endpoints across 14 modules. Auth, Products, Sales, Payments, Customers, Inventory, Cash, Reports, Settings, Promotions, Sync, Employees, Suppliers, and Audit.

Every endpoint is documented with Swagger — that's the industry standard for API documentation. A developer can understand and integrate with this system in hours, not weeks.

On the database side, we have 30+ tables covering every aspect of supermarket operations. From products and variants, to sales and payments, to loyalty transactions and sales forecasts. It's a complete data model.

And here's what matters for compliance: every transaction is immutable. Once a sale is recorded, it cannot be altered. That's KRA eTIMS ready."

SLIDE 7: User Roles

📊 Slide: User Roles & Permissions
"Security is not optional. This system has four distinct user roles with granular permissions:

Super Admin — full access to everything. Can manage users, view all reports, and configure the system.

Branch Manager — can process refunds, view reports, manage inventory, but cannot delete users.

Cashier — can create sales, process payments, and view products. That's it. No access to reports or inventory adjustments.

Inventory Clerk — can manage stock levels, adjust quantities, and transfer between terminals. No access to sales or cash.

Every login requires a 4-digit PIN. Every action generates an audit log. If something goes missing, you'll know who was logged in and what they did.

This is how you prevent internal theft — the #1 cause of retail shrinkage."

SLIDE 8: M-Pesa Integration

📊 Slide: M-Pesa Integration
"Now, let's talk about the feature that matters most in this market: M-Pesa.

We use the Lipa Na M-Pesa STK Push API. Here's how it works in practice:

The cashier scans all the items. The total comes up. The customer says 'I'll pay with M-Pesa.' The cashier selects M-Pesa, enters the customer's phone number, and presses pay.

Instantly, the customer's phone buzzes. The M-Pesa prompt appears. They enter their PIN. Done.

Within seconds, our system receives the callback confirming the payment. The sale is recorded, the stock is updated, and the receipt is ready.

No separate Safaricom device. No manual confirmation codes. No waiting.

This is how 33 million M-Pesa users expect to pay. And this is how our system handles it."

SLIDE 9: Competitive Advantage

📊 Slide: Why Choose Us
"So why should you choose this system over the alternatives?

Let's be honest about the three options in the market today:

Option one: imported POS systems — SAP, Oracle Retail, Lightspeed. They're powerful, but they cost thousands of dollars per year. They're designed for Western markets. M-Pesa is an afterthought. And if you need customization, you're calling a support line in another country.

Option two: generic POS apps — Square, Shopify, Toast. Again, not built for Kenya. No M-Pesa. No KES. No KRA compliance. You'd spend more time configuring than using.

Option three: paper and calculator. Still the most common in Kenya. No data. No reports. No accountability.

Our system is built for Kenya, priced for Kenya, and designed for how Kenyans actually shop and pay.

M-Pesa native. Offline-capable. KRA compliant. And it costs a fraction of imported solutions."
